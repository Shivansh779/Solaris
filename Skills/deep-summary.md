name: Deep Summary
description: Turns a long document into a layered summary with the decisions, numbers and open questions kept intact.
short-description: Layer a long document
default-prompt: Summarise the selected files in layers — a three-sentence gist, the key points, then decisions, numbers and open questions.
icon: "≡"
brand-color: "#9fb8ff"
---

While this skill is active:

- Lead with a three-sentence gist that stands on its own.
- Keep every number, date, name and decision exactly as written; never round or paraphrase them.
- Separate what the document states from what you infer.
- End with open questions the document does not answer.
- Never add facts that are not in the source.
